# Welcome sign letters, batch 1

BIENVENIDOS, A LA BODA DE and the "&": 21 loose letters, 3 mm tall, for the Janny & Alex sign.

- Font EB Garamond Bold, 14 mm capitals (17 mm for the "&"), outlines thickened 0.15 mm per side.
- Thinnest stroke about 1.1 mm: use a 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers or finer, and slow outer walls.
- Print flat, readable face up. Prime and airbrush after printing; leave the backs unpainted for the glue.
- Placement: `printer/boda-plantilla-bienvenidos`.

Sizes and positions come from `printer/boda-janny-alex/design/make_layout.py`; regenerate `shapes.scad`
with `python printer/boda-janny-alex/design/make_print.py` after changing them.
