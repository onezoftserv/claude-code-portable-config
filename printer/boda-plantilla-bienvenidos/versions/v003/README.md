# Welcome sign placement template, batch 1

Two 0.8 mm plates with letter-shaped holes (0.25 mm clearance per side).

| Plate | Size | Top edge from top of board | Left edge from left of board |
|---|---|---|---|
| BIENVENIDOS / A LA BODA DE | 225.7 x 67.0 mm | 259.5 mm | 192.0 mm |
| & | 39.1 x 34.8 mm | 557.9 mm | 285.2 mm |

How to use:

1. Mark the board's vertical centre line (304.8 mm from either side) lightly in pencil.
2. Put each plate so the V notches at its top and bottom sit on that line and its top edge is at the distance above.
3. Tape the plate down, glue each letter through its hole, wait 10 to 15 minutes, lift the plate straight up.

The holes follow the outside of each letter only, so nothing loose falls out of B, D, O, A or R.
The "&" plate goes on after the metallic names are in place; it sits between them.
