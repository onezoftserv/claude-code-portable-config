## Banned

Never laser these, whatever the settings:

| Material | Why |
|---|---|
| PVC, vinyl, pleather / faux leather | Releases hydrogen chloride: toxic, and corrodes the machine |
| ABS | Melts, burns, releases hydrogen cyanide |
| Polycarbonate (Lexan) | Chars and burns rather than cutting; toxic smoke |
| Fibreglass, carbon fibre composite, coated circuit board | Resin fumes and fine fibres |
| HDPE, polystyrene and polypropylene foam | Melts and catches fire |
| Anything with a mirror or chrome finish facing the beam | Reflects the beam |
| Any plastic or coated sheet you cannot identify | Unknown fumes |

Clear and light-blue acrylic do not absorb a blue diode beam and will not cut; that is a capability limit, not a hazard.
